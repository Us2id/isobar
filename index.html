        About Isobar

        A free, open-source weather map with layers, shown on a 3D globe or a flat map. It has no server: your browser asks public data services directly and keeps checking while the page is open. See the code on GitHub.

        Not an official source. Forecast values are model output and can be wrong, late or missing. For warnings you can act on, use weather.gov or your national weather service.

        Where the data comes from

        Forecast maps
        NOAA GFS, GFS Wave, GEFS Aerosols and CFS Ocean, and ECMWF IFS and AIFS open data, read directly in your browser. ECMWF data is licensed CC BY 4.0.
        Radar
        US: NOAA MRMS through the National Weather Service. World: RainViewer, limited to zoom 7 on its free plan.
        Satellite views and measurements
        NASA GIBS and NOAA nowCOAST. Many NASA products are a day or more behind.
        Lightning and alerts
        NOAA nowCOAST and the National Weather Service.
        US outlooks
        NOAA's Storm Prediction, Weather Prediction and Climate Prediction Centers, and the National Water Model.
        Cyclones and events
        GDACS and NASA EONET.
        Airports
        Iowa Environmental Mesonet.
        Forecasts for a spot
        National Weather Service inside the US. Open-Meteo everywhere else, and for soil, air, sea and model comparisons.
        Base map
        OpenFreeMap with OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data. Drawn by MapLibre GL on the globe and Leaflet on the flat map.
