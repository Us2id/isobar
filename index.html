      About Isobar

      A free, open-source weather map. It has no server: your browser asks NOAA and the other services below for the latest data and keeps checking while the page is open. See the code on GitHub.

      Not an official source. Data can be late or missing. For warnings you can act on, use weather.gov.

      Where the data comes from

      Wind flow
      NOAA GFS forecast for the current hour, worldwide. The lines follow the forecast and are sped up so you can see them. They don't show the air moving live.
      Radar
      NOAA MRMS through the National Weather Service. US only, usually a few minutes old.
      Lightning
      Strike density from NOAA nowCOAST, in 15-minute periods. Covers the Americas and the Pacific.
      Satellite
      Infrared imagery from NOAA nowCOAST. GOES updates every 5 minutes between about 11°N and 50°N across the Americas. Everywhere else uses an hourly world mosaic that can be a few hours old.
      Alerts
      National Weather Service watches and warnings, through nowCOAST and api.weather.gov. US only.
      Forecasts
      National Weather Service inside the US. Open-Meteo everywhere else.
      Base map
      OpenFreeMap with OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data.
